# Aspect Biosystems

> Aspect Biosystems is a Vancouver, British Columbia biotechnology company developing Bioprinted Tissue Therapeutics — implantable, allogeneic living tissues designed to replace, repair, or supplement biological function in the body. It operates a full-stack tissue therapeutic platform combining AI-powered microfluidic bioprinting, computational tissue design, therapeutic and hypoimmune-engineered cells, and advanced biomaterials, with a pipeline targeting metabolic and endocrine disease.
